The idea

A working network. More room for your homelab.

A homelab brings together things that were never designed as one system. Your router should make those connections easier. Lanrith is being built to take care of the networking details that otherwise become another project.

Get the foundations right.

Routing, NAT, DNS and IPv4/IPv6 belong together. We’re bringing them into one coherent setup, so the everyday connections need less troubleshooting.
